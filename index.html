The Problem

Cryogenic Infrastructure Is Becoming Harder to Operate

As quantum systems scale, cryogenic environments contain growing numbers of RF lines, thermal stages, sensors and components. Yet system understanding remains fragmented across measurements, tools and engineering disciplines.

Our Approach

An Intelligence Layer for the Cryogenic System

Q-ARNE explores how physics-based understanding and operational data can be brought together to make complex cryogenic systems easier to understand, diagnose and operate.

Observe

Bring relevant system behaviour and measurements into context.

Correlate

Connect behaviour across RF, thermal and operational signals.

Diagnose

Identify deviations and narrow down physically plausible sources.

Act

Turn complex system behaviour into clearer engineering guidance.

Where Q-ARNE Fits

Between the Infrastructure and the Qubit

Cryogenic Infrastructure

Temperatures, pressures, flows, RF chains and physical components.

Q-ARNE — System Intelligence

Physics-based understanding, correlation, diagnostics and system-level insight.

Qubit Layer — Quantum Control & Calibration

Control, measurement and calibration of the quantum processor.

Cryostat and vendor monitoring tell engineers what is happening in the physical infrastructure. Quantum-control systems focus on operating, measuring and calibrating qubits. Q-ARNE is exploring the intelligence layer between those domains — helping engineers understand why the supporting cryogenic infrastructure is behaving as it is.
